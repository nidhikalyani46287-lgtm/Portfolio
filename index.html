About

I care about privacy that's operational, not theatrical — documentation people actually use, and controls that hold up.

I trained as a lawyer in India: a five-year integrated law degree, then a couple of years in commercial practice at INDIALAW, drafting SaaS contracts and advising banks on regulatory compliance. Somewhere in the middle of all those vendor agreements and IT Act questions, I realised the part I cared about most was data — who holds it, how it moves, and what people are owed when it's mishandled.

So when I moved to Berlin in 2023, I went all-in. I spent eighteen months retooling around GDPR and the EU AI Act — ISO/IEC 27701, OneTrust, Vanta, Securiti — and rebuilt my practice from the ground up. These days I build RoPAs, run DPIAs, handle Article 28 assessments, and help organisations get their privacy operations into genuinely working order.

I'm CIPP/E in progress for 2026, AIGP next, and somewhere between A2 and B1 in German — which is to say I can mostly order coffee and increasingly argue about it. The unglamorous discipline of doing it properly is the part I like best.
